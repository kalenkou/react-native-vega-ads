vega-ads in your browser

This page runs react-native-ads-core, the ad logic of the Fire TV library react-native-vega-ads. A web video element stands in for the Vega player. The movies, ads and ad tags are the ones the Fire TV build uses.

    Try this

    1. Press Play. A pre-roll plays first. Skip shows after 5 seconds.
    2. Press Next break. "Ad in 5" shows before the break. Then a pod of ads plays. Kettlo is left out, because you saw it in the pre-roll.
    3. Pause the movie. After 1.5 seconds a pause ad shows. Back closes it.
    4. Pick Sintel. Its first mid-roll comes after a scene with an injury. The ad bar says which ad was left out.
    5. Pick "Ads as usual" to see the same movie with a timer break and repeats.

    How this differs from the TV

    • On Fire TV the player is the w3cmedia VideoPlayer. Here it is a web video element.
    • The TV prebuffers the next ad of a pod on a second surface. This page uses one surface.
    • The keys are Space (play and pause), Escape (Back) and the arrows (10 seconds).
    • The ad CDN sends no CORS headers, so the page reads its own copy of the VAST and VMAP files. The videos, images and pixels go to the same servers as on the TV.
